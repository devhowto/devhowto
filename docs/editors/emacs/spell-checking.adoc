== Most common spell checking commands

Use kbd:[M-$] (or kbd:[Esc $], or kbd:[C-[ $]) to run `ispell-word`.
It checks the spelling of the word under the cursor, and offer suggestions.
Press kbd:[i] to _insert_ the word to your personal dictionary.

[NOTE]
====
Even though the command is `ispell-word`, on my Arch Linux system, inserted/saved words are saved to `~/.hunspell_en_US`.
====

kbd:[C-;] to run `flyspell-auto-correct-previous-word` when the point is some chars _after_ the word to be corrected.
Keep pressing kbd:[C-;] to loop through the suggestions.
